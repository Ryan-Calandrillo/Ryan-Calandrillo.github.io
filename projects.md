## Featured Projects

### 1. IOL Portfolio Leave-Behind
**Bausch + Lomb Surgical, U.S. Cataract Marketing (Summer 2026)**

A single-view sales tool designed for cataract surgeons that consolidates the enVista intraocular lens portfolio (Envy, Envy Toric, Aspire, Aspire Toric, Monofocal), reinforces platform differentiation, and helps reps guide lens-to-patient decisions in the brief windows they get with surgeons.

- Authored the agency-facing project brief, defining the audience (surgeons and surgical staff), tone (clinical, precise, outcome-focused, no consumer marketing language), and front and back requirements for a two-sided printed piece
- Designed the comparison chart structure: five lens columns across the top, clinical differentiators as rows (range of vision, astigmatism correction, rotational stability, material clarity, dysphotopsia control, ideal patient), with the full-range full-toric offering carrying the visual emphasis
- Aligned with the design agency on a scannable chart approach: toric variants treated as product-family headers rather than separate columns, keeping the piece readable at a glance
- Deliverable: print-ready two-sided piece plus editable source, on B+L brand and print spec

Reference: [Bausch + Lomb Surgical, cataract portfolio](https://www.bausch.com/ecp/our-products/cataract-surgery/premium-iols/)

### 2. Field Bannerstand Order and Fulfillment System
**Bausch + Lomb Surgical, U.S. Cataract Marketing (Summer 2026)**

Built and now run the order intake and fulfillment workflow for Aspire and enVista Envy bannerstands going to field Regional Business Directors, coordinating with an outside fulfillment partner from request to delivery.

- Designed an Excel-based order tracker with dropdown validation on stand type and order status (Requested, Ordered, In Production, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled), covering all fields required to submit an order (clinic, surgeon, surgery center, account ID, requester)
- Built a Summary tab with automated SUMIFS and COUNTIFS formulas that calculate units by stand type, units by field owner, and order counts by status, with cancelled orders excluded from unit totals
- Draft the outbound communications to the field about the bannerstand program and coordinate weekly with the fulfillment partner to move orders through the pipeline

Reference: [Bausch + Lomb, corporate](https://www.bausch.com)

### 3. WHOOP Through-Brand Positioning and Voice Governance
**Rutgers Business School, AI in Marketing (Fall 2026)**

Semester-long individual project using WHOOP as a through-brand across every weekly assignment, building a locked positioning and voice governance system that stays consistent as new deliverables get layered on.

- Locked the target as the Data-Driven Optimizer, the year-round high-performance athlete, rejecting broader framings (Wellness Newcomer, generic health-conscious consumer) that would have diluted the brand's authority
- Reframed the frame of reference: WHOOP as a screenless recovery coach and a membership, not a fitness tracker or a device, with the moat built on trusted interpretation and coaching
- Defined a governing no-word list (track, wearable, transform, unlock, grind, wellness, feel, journey, biohack, streak, and others) and a set of tone rules (serious, analytical, capability-then-proof, measured authority, never hype), and run every draft against both before submission
- Building a supporting design system: cinematic and gritty mood, minimalist layout, soft lighting, muted palette, with real feature names (Recovery, Strain, Sleep, Healthspan, WHOOP Age) treated as proper nouns

References: [WHOOP](https://www.whoop.com) | [Rutgers Business School](https://www.business.rutgers.edu)
