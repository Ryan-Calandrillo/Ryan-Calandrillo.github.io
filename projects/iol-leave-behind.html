IOL Portfolio Leave-Behind

Bausch + Lomb Surgical, U.S. Cataract Marketing · Summer 2026

Situation

Sales representatives are asked to position the full B+L IOL portfolio and support cataract surgeons in lens selection, often within brief windows of a surgeon's time. Existing resources did not provide a single tool that consolidated the portfolio into one view, differentiated the platform, and guided the lens-to-patient decision at a glance.

Approach

Authored the agency-facing project brief that defined the entire piece: audience (cataract surgeons and surgical staff), tone (clinical, precise, outcome-focused with no consumer marketing language), and the requirements for both sides of a two-sided printed leave-behind.

Designed the back-side comparison chart structure: five lens columns across the top, clinical differentiators as rows (range of vision, astigmatism correction, rotational stability, material clarity, dysphotopsia control, ideal patient). The chart is built to let a surgeon answer three questions at a glance: which lens do I use, why does it perform well, and what trade-off am I managing.

Key Decisions

  • Rejected a layout that would have added a separate "Toric available" label or extra toric columns. Aligned with the design agency to treat toric variants as product-family headers instead, keeping the chart scannable at a glance.
  • Led with the full range of vision offering in the first column position to anchor the portfolio around platform differentiation, not lowest-price entry.
  • Held the tone line against consumer marketing language throughout: the piece is written for surgeons, not for patients, and the copy stays in clinical register.
  • Structured the front side around a lens or optical diagram as the primary graphic with short supporting copy, so the piece reads as a tool rather than a brochure.

Outcome

Print-ready two-sided piece plus editable source, on B+L brand and print spec. The leave-behind is on the sales rep tool shelf as a portfolio-level differentiation piece.
